# How to cite the presentation slides

Replace the bracketed fields with the information shown on the archive page.

中文推荐格式：

> 曹溪能（Xixi Cao），《[幻灯片标题]》，文献分享幻灯片，[分享日期]，
> Xixi Research Atlas，[幻灯片 PDF 永久链接]（访问日期：YYYY-MM-DD）。

English:

> Cao, Xineng (Xixi Cao). *[Presentation title]*. Article Share presentation
> slides, [presentation date]. Xixi Research Atlas. [permanent PDF URL]
> (accessed YYYY-MM-DD).

Also cite the underlying paper using its DOI, journal, or arXiv record. If the
slides are adapted, identify the changes and include “adapted from Cao (date).”
Third-party figures and quotations require their own source attribution and
permission analysis.
